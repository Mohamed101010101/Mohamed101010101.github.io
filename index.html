COMPUTATIONAL COMPENDIUM

Flagship Oncology Evidence Engine

A hermetic, publication-grade research compendium engineered according to rOpenSci and ICMJE guidelines to synthesize randomized trial evidence in advanced non-small cell lung cancer.

Frequentist Network Meta-Analysis Engine for First-Line Advanced NSCLC

Resolving the comparative efficacy and toxicity profiles of 12 first-line immune checkpoint inhibitor combinations against histology-stratified platinum doublet chemotherapy without direct head-to-head trial availability.

✓ Laplacian Inversion: Electrical network random-walk synthesis preserving multi-arm covariance.
✓ Inconsistency Diagnostics: Node-splitting back-calculation & NetHeat spectral leverage matrices.
✓ Monte Carlo Simulations: 10,000-scenario ranking iterations for P-scores and SUCRA distributions.
✓ Clinical Valuation: ASCO / ESMO Minimal Clinically Important Difference (MCID) frontiers.

MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATIONS

Methodological Matrix & Biostatistical Rigor

Translating high-dimensional RCT parameters into coherent evidence structures via graph theory, spectral matrices, and epidemiological bias adjustments.

🧬

Graph-Theoretical Synthesis

  • Frequentist weighted graph modeling via electrical network analogy
  • Laplacian matrix pseudo-inversion for exact multi-arm variance preservation
  • Component network meta-analysis (CNMA) additive interaction testing
  • Restricted maximum likelihood (REML) heterogeneity estimation
🔍

Inconsistency Auditing

  • Node-splitting back-calculation separating direct from indirect trial loops
  • NetHeat spectral leverage matrix diagnostics identifying hot loops
  • Design-by-treatment interaction decomposition across trial designs
  • Leave-one-out sensitivity stability audits across all 24 RCTs
⚖️

Sparse Data & Bias

  • Copas non-ignorable selection models for missing outcome data
  • Contour-enhanced funnel plots distinguishing bias from heterogeneity
  • Firth penalized likelihood regression for zero-event safety arms
  • Full Cochrane RoB 2.0 and ROBINS-I appraisal architectures
📊

Clinical Decision Frontiers

  • ASCO / ESMO Minimal Clinically Important Difference (MCID) benchmarking
  • Bivariate benefit-risk planes integrating OS hazard ratios and grade 3-5 ORs
  • 10,000-scenario Monte Carlo simulations for treatment ranking
  • CINeMA certainty of evidence and GRADE evidence profiles
